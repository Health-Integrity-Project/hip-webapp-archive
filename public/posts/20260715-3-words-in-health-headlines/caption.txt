Health headlines love big words, and most of them promise less than they sound like.

A quick decoder for the three that do the most work: linked, risk, and causes. Each one comes with a question that tells you how seriously to take the claim.

The sneakiest is risk. "Doubles your risk" is technically true even when it means going from 1 in 10,000 to 2 in 10,000. That's the gap between relative risk (the scary percentage) and absolute risk (what happens to you). A claim that only gives you the percentage is hiding the ball.

Save this for the next time a headline tries to scare or sell you.

Not medical advice, just a way to read the news better.
